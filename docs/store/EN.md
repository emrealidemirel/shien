## Name
Shien

## App Store subtitle
Plan your day. Remember more.

## Google Play short description
Plan reminders, set repeats and keep your day organized.

## Description
Keep track of daily tasks, important dates and things you want to do with Shien. See your plan in a clear layout, review what you have completed and focus on what comes next.

• See upcoming reminders in Today and Plan.
• Create one-time or recurring reminders with daily, weekly, monthly and yearly schedules.
• Add multiple times, advance alerts and snooze options.
• Organize reminders with notes, categories, tags and priority marks.
• Review completed tasks in your history.
• Reach your next reminder from a home screen widget.
• Choose a notification sound and switch between light and dark themes.
• Work offline as a guest or use an account to sync across devices.
• Export and import your records as JSON files.
• Switch between Turkish and English.

Notification delivery depends on device permissions, silent/focus modes and battery settings. Sync requires an internet connection; the app does not send remote notifications while it is closed.
